Check compatibility before purchasing: Orfeusz Gateway supports more than one USB–UART adapter. Do not choose by chip name or a generic product description alone: check the compatibility list and verify the exact breakout board. The CTS, GND and RTS signals must be physically accessible for the radio interface. A basic adapter exposing only TX/RX/GND, without CTS/RTS, is not suitable. “Not verified” does not automatically mean incompatible.

The 470 Ω potentiometer is used for audio level adjustment as shown in the diagram.

Important safety note: Before assembly, verify the connector viewing direction, the actual pinout of the radio socket and the USB–UART adapter labels. Do not confuse the Con1 numbering with the pin layout viewed from the solder side of the plug. Check all ground connections and make sure there are no short circuits before powering up. This document describes the supplied diagram; it does not replace electrical checks on your own hardware.
