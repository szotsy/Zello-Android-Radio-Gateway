FTDI compatibility: The VID/PID (0403:6001) identifies an FTDI FT232R device. Before purchasing, check that the specific adapter exposes both CTS and RTS pins. A basic adapter that only exposes TX/RX/GND and has no CTS/RTS pins is not suitable.

The 470 Ω potentiometer is used for audio level adjustment as shown in the diagram.

Important safety note: Before assembly, verify the connector viewing direction, the actual pinout of the radio socket and the FTDI adapter labels. Do not confuse the Con1 numbering with the pin layout viewed from the solder side of the plug. Check all ground connections and make sure there are no short circuits before powering up. This document describes the supplied diagram; it does not replace electrical checks on your own hardware.
