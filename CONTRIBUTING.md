# Contributing to Orfeusz Gateway

Thank you for considering a contribution.

## Licence

Unless a different arrangement is agreed in writing before the contribution
is accepted, contributions submitted for inclusion in this repository are
intended to be distributed under the repository's **GPL-3.0-only** licence.

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to submit
it under these terms. Do not submit code, documentation, images, fonts, or
other material copied from third parties unless its licence permits the
intended use and the required notices are supplied.

## Third-party material

- Preserve existing copyright and licence notices.
- Identify new dependencies and their exact versions and licences.
- Update THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md when adding, removing, or changing a dependency.
- Do not commit secrets, signing keys, credentials, or private user data.

## Changes and testing

Describe the purpose and user-visible impact of the change. Include the
devices, Android versions, and USB-UART adapters tested where relevant.
Clearly identify untested hardware capabilities.

## Branding

The GPL permits code forks under its terms but does not grant rights to use
official Orfeusz Gateway branding. See TRADEMARK.md.
